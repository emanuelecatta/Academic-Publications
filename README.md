- Relativistic limit on the AdS^3 × S^3 × S^3 × S^1 string worldsheet

Abstract: We study the relativistic limit of the worldsheet S-matrix of  AdS^3 × S^3 × S^3 × S^1 strings in the presence of mixed Ramond–Ramond (RR) and Neveu-Schwarz–NeveuSchwarz (NS-NS) flux and complete the S-matrix bootstrap including the dressing factors for fundamental particles and bound states. Our results agree with the relativistic limit of the recent proposal arXiv:2512.07721.
